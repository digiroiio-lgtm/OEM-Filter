OVERVIEW

Engineered for Cabin Air Quality

Cabin air filters protect vehicle occupants from dust, pollen and particulate drawn through the HVAC system. Activated-carbon variants add odor and gas adsorption for markets that require it.

We produce flat-panel and contoured cabin filter formats matched to each OE housing profile, verified against reference samples for dimensional fit before release.
